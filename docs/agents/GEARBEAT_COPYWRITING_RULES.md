# GearBeat Copywriting Rules

Our messaging must match the premium, professional, and trustworthy nature of our platform. Unclear or exaggerated claims damage our reputation, while clean, minimal copy builds user confidence.

---

## 1. Tone and Voice Guidelines

*   **Premium & Minimal**: Avoid cluttered explanations. Use strong, direct verbs and elegant adjectives (e.g., "Handcrafted analog consoles," "Acoustically tuned spaces").
*   **Clear & Direct**: Eliminate tech jargon where simple copy works. Speak directly to musicians, producers, and studio owners.
*   **Trustworthy & Professional**: Never make bold, unsupported promises. State facts clearly.

---

## 2. Mandatory Rules for Arabic & English Labels

GearBeat is a bilingual platform tailored to the Saudi/Middle Eastern market and global users. All UI elements must maintain text parity.

*   **Translation Sincerity**:
    *   Never use literal translations that lose their local context or cultural nuance.
    *   Avoid using automatic Google Translate outputs for core user interfaces. Use verified local terminology (e.g., use "حجز استوديو" for "Book Studio" instead of literal terms that sound robotic).
*   **Text Length Parity**:
    *   Arabic copy is typically 20-30% longer than English equivalents.
    *   Always design CSS flexboxes and grid containers with extra line height and character tolerance to avoid text truncation or overlapping elements in RTL mode.
*   **Format Consistency**:
    *   Keep punctuation, numbers, and capitalization consistent across views.
    *   When displaying dates or currency values, format them correctly based on the active locale (e.g., SR/SAR vs. ريال).

---

## 3. Avoid Unsupported Claims (Legal & Brand Safety)

To prevent customer disputes and compliance issues, do not write copy that promises absolute outcomes. Follow these copy boundaries:

*   **Payment Claims**:
    *   *Incorrect*: "100% Guaranteed Instant Refund."
    *   *Correct*: "Refunds are processed according to the cancellation policy."
*   **Gear Condition Claims**:
    *   *Incorrect*: "Every instrument is brand new and completely flawless."
    *   *Correct*: "All gear is checked and verified by GearBeat Certified technicians."
*   **Booking Claims**:
    *   *Incorrect*: "Double-booking is physically impossible."
    *   *Correct*: "Our atomic reservation engine ensures your chosen slot is reserved exclusively for you."
*   **Academy & Earnings Claims**:
    *   *Incorrect*: "Become a certified producer and double your studio income in 30 days."
    *   *Correct*: "Learn professional studio techniques from certified industry experts."
